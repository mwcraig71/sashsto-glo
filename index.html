Getting There

PKL Park sits just west of Unity Park, a mile from the SASHTO host hotels. The conference runs a free shuttle, and the walk along the river is a good one if you would rather stretch your legs.

Take the Conference Shuttle

Every 30 min · from 5:30 PM · no charge

SASHTO runs a free shuttle from the AC Hotel Greenville. Departures every thirty minutes beginning at 5:30 PM, with the last outbound run at 9:00 PM. Return trips keep going until everyone who wants a ride is back downtown — no need to plan the trip home.

Walk the Swamp Rabbit Trail

1.1 mi · 20–25 min · flat

Pick up the trail at Reedy View Drive and follow the Reedy River north through Falls Park and Unity Park. Paved, flat, and shaded most of the way. Best done outbound in daylight.

Walking Directions

Drive or Rideshare

1.3 mi · about 5 min

South Academy Street to North Markley, then South Hudson to Mayberry. Free parking on site. Rideshare is the easiest way back downtown at the end of the night.

Driving Directions
Getting back: the shuttle runs return trips from PKL Park until everyone who wants a ride is downtown. If you walked out, take the shuttle back — the trail is unlit through stretches of Unity Park.
